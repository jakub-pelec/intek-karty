# Idea: extra RNG / dice

**Status:** parked on purpose. Separate future discussion.

**Problem this would try to solve:** ranked that is fully determined once both lineups are known (except reveal order, which is already fixed 1→6). Extra rolls could add swing after queue.

**What was already decided for the current pass**

- After queue, the player still **does not act**.
- **Luck on ranked is acceptable in principle** — a worse lineup may win later because of chance.
- **Not this pass.** Uncertainty for now is only the **hidden enemy lineup** as it flips. No extra die, coin, or mid-reveal event.

## Why it was split off

Hidden-enemy-board and dice are different promises. The first is “I built against a range of tables.” The second is “the table can ignore both binders.” Mixing them in one brief makes it impossible to tell whether a comeback was interaction or a roll.

## Future discussion must answer

1. Does the roll change **the path** (which synergies look exciting) or **the winner**?
2. Ranked, practice, or both?
3. Is the roll public at match lock (seeded, replayable, fair to inspect) or revealed live?
4. What is still the binder’s job if a roll can beat a better collection?
5. How this plays with cross-board scoring (already more swingy) without turning the reveal into noise.

## Explicit non-goals until rediscovered

- Player-chosen timing of the roll (that would break watch-the-reveal).
- Using dice as the way to nerf OP decks (that is `deck-construction.md`).
